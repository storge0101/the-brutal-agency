Design a dark, cinematic, brutalist-style website for a 360 digital marketing agency called:

The website should feel bold, disruptive, confident, and performance-driven, similar to a creative tech startup combined with a growth lab.

• Brutalist typography
• Large kinetic headlines
• Cinematic gradients and lighting
• Minimal but powerful layouts
• Motion-driven interactions

Inspired by:

• creative studios

• performance growth labs

Design elements should include:

• hover effects
